# About Us

Welcome to **All Things Video Games**.

We built this site as a simple, organized place to explore video game genres, discover titles, and read reviews.

## What you can find here

- Genre-based game pages
- Game lists and highlights
- Reviews and recommendations

## Our goal

Make it easier for players to find games they enjoy and learn more about the games they love.
