# History of Video Games

Video games have grown from simple experiments on lab computers into one of the largest entertainment industries in the world. This page highlights key eras, major milestones, and influential games that helped shape modern gaming.

## 1950s–1970s: Foundations and Arcades

- **Early experiments:** Games like *Tennis for Two* (1958) and *Spacewar!* (1962) proved interactive computer entertainment was possible.
- **Arcade breakthrough:** *Pong* (1972) brought gaming to mainstream audiences.
- **Home consoles begin:** The Magnavox Odyssey (1972) introduced the idea of playing games on a TV at home.

### Influential games

- *Pong* (1972)
- *Space Invaders* (1978)
- *Asteroids* (1979)

## 1980s: Home Console Boom and Crash

- **Golden age of arcades:** Fast-paced score-chasing games dominated arcades.
- **Console growth:** The Atari 2600 made cartridge-based home gaming popular.
- **1983 crash:** Market oversaturation and low-quality releases hurt the North American industry.
- **Recovery led by Nintendo:** The NES (1985 in NA) restored consumer trust and redefined console standards.

### Influential games

- *Pac-Man* (1980)
- *Donkey Kong* (1981)
- *Super Mario Bros.* (1985)
- *The Legend of Zelda* (1986)

## 1990s: 16-bit to 3D Revolution

- **16-bit era rivalry:** Sega and Nintendo pushed rapid innovation in game design and marketing.
- **3D graphics take off:** New hardware enabled richer worlds and camera-based movement.
- **Disc media rise:** CD-based systems increased storage for audio, cinematics, and larger games.
- **Genre expansion:** RPGs, survival horror, and 3D platformers saw major growth.

### Influential games

- *Sonic the Hedgehog* (1991)
- *Doom* (1993)
- *Final Fantasy VII* (1997)
- *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* (1998)

## 2000s: Online Play and Modern Franchises

- **Online multiplayer becomes standard:** Broadband internet made competitive and cooperative online gaming mainstream.
- **Console ecosystems mature:** Xbox, PlayStation 2/3, and Nintendo Wii expanded audience reach in different ways.
- **Digital distribution starts scaling:** PC storefronts and console marketplaces changed how games were sold.
- **Large franchises dominate:** Shooter, sports, and open-world series became annual or multi-year tentpoles.

### Influential games

- *Halo: Combat Evolved* (2001)
- *World of Warcraft* (2004)
- *Wii Sports* (2006)
- *Minecraft* (2009)

## 2010s: Streaming, Indie Growth, and Live Service

- **Indie development expands:** Easier publishing tools helped smaller studios reach global audiences.
- **Streaming culture rises:** Platforms like Twitch and YouTube turned gameplay into spectator entertainment.
- **Live-service model grows:** Frequent updates, seasonal content, and in-game events became common.
- **Cross-platform discussions accelerate:** Communities increasingly expected unified social play across devices.

### Influential games

- *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* (2015)
- *Overwatch* (2016)
- *Fortnite* (2017)
- *The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild* (2017)

## 2020s: Cross-Platform Era and New Tech

- **Hybrid and portable power:** Devices like the Nintendo Switch and PC handhelds broadened where people play.
- **Cross-play and cross-progression:** More games support shared progress and multiplayer across platforms.
- **Subscription libraries:** Services provide access to broad catalogs instead of individual purchases only.
- **AI, accessibility, and tools:** Development pipelines and player features continue to evolve quickly.

### Influential games

- *Elden Ring* (2022)
- *Baldur's Gate 3* (2023)
- *Helldivers 2* (2024)

## Why this history matters

Understanding the history of video games helps explain modern trends in design, business models, and community culture. Many current ideas—online multiplayer, open worlds, modding communities, and esports—were built through decades of iteration across these eras.
