# Popular Video Game Content Creators

Use this resource to discover well-known creators across different gaming communities.

## Variety and Commentary

- [PewDiePie](https://www.youtube.com/@PewDiePie) — Variety gameplay and gaming commentary.
- [jacksepticeye](https://www.youtube.com/@jacksepticeye) — High-energy variety games and playthroughs.
- [Markiplier](https://www.youtube.com/@markiplier) — Narrative and indie game playthroughs.

## Strategy and Competitive

- [Faker (T1)](https://www.twitch.tv/faker) — League of Legends professional gameplay.
- [Shroud](https://www.twitch.tv/shroud) — FPS-focused competitive streams.
- [TheScore esports](https://www.youtube.com/@theScoreesports) — Esports stories, highlights, and analysis.

## Family-Friendly and Creative

- [DanTDM](https://www.youtube.com/@DanTDM) — Family-friendly gaming content and Minecraft videos.
- [CaptainSparklez](https://www.youtube.com/@CaptainSparklez) — Minecraft-focused videos and community content.
- [KreekCraft](https://www.youtube.com/@KreekCraft) — Roblox gameplay and livestreams.

## How to Discover More Creators

- Search by game title + "creator" or "stream highlights".
- Check your favorite game's official esports or community page.
- Follow platform tags on YouTube and Twitch for your favorite genres.
- Look for creators with active moderation and clear community rules.
