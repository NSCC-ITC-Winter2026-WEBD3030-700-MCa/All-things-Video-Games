## Sports

Games based on real-world sports or sports-style competition, from simulation to arcade.

- **EA Sports FC 24** — PC / PS4 / PS5 / Xbox One / Series / Switch — 2023  
  Modern football/soccer sim (formerly FIFA branding).
- **NBA 2K24** — PC / PS4 / PS5 / Xbox One / Series / Switch — 2023  
  Basketball sim with career and franchise modes.
- **Rocket League** — PC / PS4 / PS5 / Xbox One / Series / Switch — 2015  
  “Car soccer” with a competitive scene and fast matches.
- **Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 1 + 2** — PC / PS4 / PS5 / Xbox One / Series / Switch — 2020  
  Skateboarding arcade classic remade.
