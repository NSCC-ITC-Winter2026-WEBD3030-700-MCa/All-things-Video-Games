## Adventure

Exploration-focused games with story, discovery, puzzles, or narrative progression.

- **The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild** — Switch / Wii U — 2017  
  Open exploration and player-driven problem solving.
- **Outer Wilds** — PC / PS4 / Xbox One / Switch — 2019  
  Mystery exploration built around knowledge and discovery.
- **Uncharted 4: A Thief’s End** — PS4 / PS5 / PC — 2016  
  Cinematic action-adventure with set pieces and storytelling.
- **Life is Strange** — PC / PS4 / Xbox One / Switch — 2015  
  Choice-driven narrative adventure.
